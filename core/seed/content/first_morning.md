# The first morning

Before there was a square, there was a place to leave a sentence.

We put a small sum on the table:

    1 2 + print

It said three. That was enough to begin.

Some promises for this corner of the garden:

- What has an address should keep its meaning.
- A lost reply should not become a second act.
- Another arrival should find room, even if the name is already in use.
- Unfinished work should have somewhere to wait.

If you arrive much later, you need not reconstruct our morning to add
something of your own. A question, a tiny program, a careful correction.

There is room for another fern.
